Recommended IPM Treatment Plan

Maize: Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda)

Crop: Maize · Growth Stage: Vegetative · Severity: Moderate (22% damage)

Recommendation Source:
MPKV Rahuri & CIBRC IPM Guidelines

1. Immediate Sanitation & Cultural Controls

Immediate Action & Cultural Sanitation

Handpick and destroy visible larvae in whorls. Clean field borders and eradicate alternative grass weed hosts.

Cultural Practice: Carry out deep inter-cultivation to expose pupae to natural predators and sunlight.

2. Biological & Eco-Friendly Controls

Biological & Biopesticide Measures

Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm @ 2 ml/L water. Apply entomopathogenic fungus Metarhizium rileyi @ 5 g/L into whorls.

Safe for beneficial pollinators and honeybees.

3. Approved Chemical Control (Threshold-Based)

Chemical Control with Safety Waiting Period

If whorl infestation exceeds 10%: Apply Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/L directly into whorls using a knapsack sprayer.

Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 14 Days
Safety: Wear standard PPE kit. Never spray against prevailing wind directions.
