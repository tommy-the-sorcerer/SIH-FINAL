### 1.1. A Novel Pigeonpea Leaf Dataset for Detection and Classification of Pigeonpea Leaf Diseases (PPLD)
- **Primary Investigators:** Vanita Doddamani, Prof. G.G. Rajput
- **Affiliation:** Department of Studies in Computer Science, Rani Channamma University, Belagavi, Karnataka, India
- **Field Collection Location:** Agriculture College, Hittinahalli Campus, Vijayapur, Karnataka (Agro-climatic zone contiguous with Southern Maharashtra border districts: Solapur, Sangli)
- **Publication Venue:** Mendeley Data / Elsevier
- **Persistent DOI:** `https://doi.org/10.17632/bd553pdtny.1`
- **DataCite Registration:** `10.17632/bd553pdtny.1` (Verified Active)
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`)
- **Camera Sensor:** Sony Cyber-Shot Digital Camera under natural sunlight
- **Classes:** 
  1. Cercospora Leaf Spot (*Cercospora cajani*) — 336 images
  2. Sterility Mosaic Disease (SMD) — 292 images
  3. Healthy Canopy — 196 images
  4. Leaf Webber (*Grapholita critica*) — 146 images
- **Total Validated Specimens:** 1,000 images ($256 \times 256$ px, RGB JPEG)
